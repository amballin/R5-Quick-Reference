Guided manual registration
Before the first slot
  1. Save an independent pre-configuration camera-settings backup.
  2. Start from a normal shooting mode such as Fv, P, Tv, Av, or M—never from a recalled C1, C2, or C3 state.
  3. Keep Custom shooting mode Auto update disabled while setting shared controls, My Menu, and baseline values.

Loading the saved C1–C3 assignments…

After all three slots
  1. Recall C1, C2, and C3 one at a time and rescan each assigned profile.
  2. Confirm Auto update is still disabled and recheck earlier slots after completing a later slot.
  3. Save a separate final camera-settings backup and keep the pre-configuration backup intact.
