Recoverable holding area

          
Deleted Cards

          
Cards moved here are inactive but retain their exact source and immutable identity. Restore requires an exact diff review and validation.

        

        
      

      
No permanent delete · The normal editor can restore these cards but cannot purge them.

A never-saved browser draft is discarded from Review & Build and never enters Deleted Cards. Cards referenced by C1–C3 assignments, another card, an appendix, or another registered structured relationship are not eligible until those references are removed.
