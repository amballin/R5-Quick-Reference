Recoverable holding area

Deleted Cards

Cards moved here are inactive but retain their exact source and immutable identity. Restore requires an exact diff review and validation.

No permanent delete · The normal editor can restore these cards but cannot purge them.

How to move a card here

  1. In Profiles, uncheck Include in released card bundle.
  2. Review and save that release-state change.
  3. Open Profile actions, choose Move to Deleted Cards, then review and confirm.

A never-saved browser draft is discarded from Review & Build and never enters Deleted Cards. Cards referenced by C1–C3 assignments, another card, an appendix, or another registered structured relationship are not eligible until those references are removed. Permanent reference cards are never eligible.
